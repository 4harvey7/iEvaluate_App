WHAT THIS ROLE DOES
  The Department Head reviews the faculty in their OWN department, spots who is
  struggling, and takes formal action through an intervention report. Two
  properties matter more than anything cosmetic:
    1. SCOPE  - a head sees only their own department, never the whole college.
    2. EFFECT - an intervention they raise reaches the instructor, and an
                instructor cannot dismiss it.
  This role can also deactivate an instructor's account, so that path is tested
  explicitly.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO READ THIS DOCUMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Text in "double quotes" appears on screen exactly as written.
* Names in [square brackets] are database tables or services to check when a
  step needs back-end verification.
* Each step has its own expected result, so a failed case identifies WHICH
  step failed.
* "Actual Result" is blank where the case has not been run.
!! WARNING !!
  Cases marked [LIVE DATA] create real intervention records against real
  instructors, or deactivate a real account. Run them on staging, or on a
  throwaway instructor account, and never during a live evaluation period.
  TC-D08 (deactivating an instructor) is the highest-impact action in this
  module.

SIGN IN AS DEPARTMENT HEAD AND READ THE EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW

Preconditions

App on the Login screen. Account is active with role DEPARTMENT_HEAD or DEAN, and IS assigned to a department.

Test Data

View only. Tables [department_table], [user_info].

Steps
1. Sign in with the department head credentials.
Expected: Routes to the department head landing screen, NOT the instructor or admin dashboard. Bottom tabs read "Overview", "Faculty", "Subjects", "Reports".
2. Confirm the role boundary.
Expected: There is NO route to any SAO Admin screen - no Users, no Personnel, no System Settings. A head who can reach an admin screen is a FAIL.
3. Read the header and leadership area.
Expected: Shows "Executive Overview" / "Department Overview" and identifies the department this head leads, under "Department Leadership".
4. Read the summary figures.
Expected: Shows "Department Average", "Active Faculty" and "Total Forms Processed". The average is qualified by "Based on N of M faculty", so a partly-evaluated department is not mistaken for a complete one.
5. Confirm the department average only covers THIS department.
Expected: The figure reconciles with this department's instructors only. No instructor from another department contributes.
6. Read "Department Growth".
Expected: Subtitle reads "Weighted mean across recent academic terms." with a value per term. With no history it reads "No historical data available yet". 6b. Read the "Forms Collected" panel and cross-check it against [sast_all_raw_data_survey] for this department and term. The count equals the number of forms actually SCANNED, not the number already folded into the scores. Where some are still queued it adds "n not yet reflected in the average". The old card read "Total Forms Processed" and printed the aggregated figure, which understated a real department by 51 forms and gave the head no way to tell a quiet week from a stalled n8n pipeline.
7. Read "Department Voice (AI Analysis)".
Expected: Subtitle reads "Aggregated themes from all student comments across the college." With no data it reads "No feedback data yet for this term."
8. Read the "Action Required" section.
Expected: Subtitle reads "Faculty and curriculum signals that need review." With nothing flagged it reads "No critical issues detected."
9. Note every instructor listed under "Action Required", then open the "Reports" tab and compare against "Pending Action Required".
Expected: The two lists MATCH. The dashboard must never show a flagged instructor that Reports counts as handled.
10. File an intervention report for one flagged instructor, then return to the dashboard and pull to refresh.
Expected: That instructor's alert is gone and the bell count drops by one. An alert the head has already acted on this term must not keep demanding action.
11. Confirm the alert is suppressed for THIS term only.
Expected: The suppression is scoped to the active term. If the instructor is still below the line in a later term, the alert is expected to return - that is the 3-strike rule working, not a regression.
12. Sign in as a department head with NO department assigned.
Expected: The screen explains the account has no department rather than showing an empty or misleading dashboard, and does not crash.
Expected Result

The head lands on their own overview with no admin access, and every figure is scoped to their department alone.

Actual Result

Title / Business Scenario

EXECUTIVE SETTINGS - PROFILE, ALERTS AND SECURITY

Preconditions

Signed in as department head, Settings opened from the drawer. Access to the account's inbox.

Test Data

A new password of at least six characters, NOT recorded in this document.

Steps
1. Open "Account Settings" from the drawer.
Expected: Screen titled "Executive Settings" (briefly "Loading executive settings…"), showing "Executive Profile" and identifying the role as "Dept Head" with the department and "College".
2. Tap "Edit Personal Details" / "Edit Executive Profile", change the name and save.
Expected: The profile saves and the new name appears in the drawer header.
3. Find the "Executive Intelligence" section.
Expected: Subtitle reads "Choose which department signals should notify you." with two toggles: "Alert when instructor rating drops below 3.0." and "Alert on detected negative student feedback."
4. Toggle each alert off, leave Settings, and return.
Expected: Both settings persist. On failure the message reads "Failed to update" naming the setting, rather than silently reverting.
5. With the rating alert ON, arrange for an instructor to fall below 3.0.
Expected: The head is alerted, and the instructor appears flagged on the roster and under "Action Required".
6. With the rating alert OFF, repeat.
Expected: No alert is raised, confirming the toggle actually controls behaviour rather than only appearing to.
7. Tap "Edit Email" and submit a valid new address.
Expected: Reads "Enter your new email address. You will receive a confirmation link.", then "Email updated! Check your inbox for confirmation." The old address works until the link is clicked.
8. Tap "Edit Password" and submit mismatched passwords.
Expected: Refused with a mismatch message.
9. Submit a valid new password with the WRONG current password.
Expected: Refused. The password does not change.
10. Submit a fully valid password change.
Expected: Succeeds and the session ends, requiring a fresh sign-in. On failure it reads "Failed to update password."
11. Open "Deactivate My Account", then CANCEL.
Expected: A warning explains the account can only be restored by the SAO Admin, and cancelling leaves the account active.
Expected Result

The head can maintain their own profile, the two alert toggles genuinely control whether they are notified, and every password rule is enforced.
