WHAT THIS ROLE DOES
  The SAO Staff member is the person who physically scans completed paper SAST
  evaluation forms into the system. Their work is a pipeline:

      scan a form  ->  it queues locally  ->  it uploads to n8n  ->
      anything n8n cannot read is flagged  ->  staff corrects it  ->  it syncs

  This document tests that pipeline, because that is where the role's real work
  and its real risk of data loss sit. Everything else is secondary.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO READ THIS DOCUMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Text in "double quotes" appears on screen exactly as written.
* Names in [square brackets] are database tables, edge functions or n8n
  webhooks to check when a step needs back-end verification.
* Each step has its own expected result.
* "Actual Result" is blank where the case has not been run.

!! DATA-LOSS WARNING !!
  A scanned form represents a real student's evaluation that exists on paper
  only once. Cases marked [LIVE DATA] upload or discard real records. The
  single most important property in this whole module is that A SCAN IS NEVER
  SILENTLY LOST. Several cases below exist only to prove that.

TC-S01  SIGN IN AS SAO STAFF AND READ THE SHIFT DASHBOARD
================================================================================
Preconditions : App on the Login screen. Account is active with role SAO_STAFF.
Test Data     : View only.

STEPS
 1. Sign in with the SAO staff credentials.
    Expected: Routes to the gatherer screen titled "Dashboard", with tabs
              "Dashboard", "Scanner", "Validation" and "Sync Queue".
 2. Confirm the role boundary.
    Expected: There is NO route to any SAO Admin screen - no Users, no
              Personnel, no System Settings, no Security Audit Logs. A staff
              account that can reach an admin screen is a FAIL.
 3. Read the term shown in the app bar.
    Expected: It is the active term set by the SAO admin.
 4. Read the "Shift Activity" section.
    Expected: Subtitle reads "Live database and local queue totals." with four
              counters: "ENTRIES TODAY / submitted today", "PENDING / not yet
              synced", "SUCCESS / synced this session", and the overall survey
              count "gathered this term".
 5. Confirm each counter means what its label says.
    Expected: ENTRIES TODAY counts today's submissions; PENDING counts scans
              held locally and not yet uploaded; SUCCESS counts uploads in
              this session; the term counter matches the "Files Scanned"
              figure on the SAO admin dashboard for the same term.
 6. Scan one form (see TC-S02), then return to the dashboard.
    Expected: PENDING increases by one. After a successful sync, PENDING falls
              and SUCCESS rises.

Expected Result : The staff member lands on their own dashboard with no admin
                  access, and the four shift counters track the real state of
                  the local queue and the database.
Actual Result   : PASS on steps 1, 3 and 4 (observed 31/08/2026). Dashboard
                  showed the term "2nd Semester, 2027-2028", "Shift Activity"
                  with "Live database and local queue totals.", the counters
                  "0 ENTRIES TODAY / submitted today", "0 PENDING / not yet
                  synced", "0 SUCCESS / synced this session" and "152 ...
                  gathered this term", plus the "IMPORT FROM GOOGLE SHEETS"
                  button and the four tabs.
                  The term count of 152 MATCHES the "152 Files Scanned" on the
                  SAO admin dashboard, so step 5's cross-check passes for that
                  counter.
                  Steps 2 and 6 not executed.

TC-S02  SCAN A COMPLETED FORM - HAPPY PATH                         [LIVE DATA]
================================================================================
Preconditions : On the "Scanner" tab. Camera permission granted. One completed
                paper SAST form. Good lighting and a plain background.
Test Data     : One real completed form. Note its Student ID and instructor
                before scanning, so the result can be checked.

STEPS
 1. Open the "Scanner" tab for the first time.
    Expected: The app requests camera permission. Granting it starts the
              preview; refusing it shows a clear explanation, not a black
              screen.
 2. Read the on-screen guidance.
    Expected: Shows "FIT SHORT BOND PAPER INSIDE THE FRAME" (or LONG, matching
              the selected size), "Leave a gap between paper and frame edges",
              and "Hold steady  -  Plain background  -  Good lighting".
 3. Select the paper size matching the form.
    Expected: "Short Bond" and "Long Bond" are both selectable, and the frame
              guidance updates to the chosen size.
 4. Hold the device in landscape.
    Expected: The app asks for portrait, reading "PLEASE HOLD PORTRAIT" and
              "The scanner works best in upright mode". It does not capture a
              sideways image.
 5. Align the form in the frame and hold still.
    Expected: Status progresses through "FOCUSING…" and "HOLD STEADY…" to
              "TAP TO CAPTURE".
 6. Tap to capture.
    Expected: The image is taken and analysed, showing "ANALYZING
              SHARPNESS...".
 7. Accept the capture with "USE PHOTO".
    Expected: The scan is accepted and added to the local queue. The dashboard
              "PENDING" counter increases by one.
 8. Confirm the scan is queued BEFORE any upload.
    Expected: The scan exists locally even with no network. It is not lost if
              the app is closed before syncing.

Expected Result : A well-presented form can be captured with clear guidance
                  throughout, and the accepted scan is stored locally before
                  any upload is attempted.
Actual Result   :
Comments        : Step 8 is the data-safety guarantee. If a captured scan can
                  vanish because the network was down, that is the most serious
                  defect this module can have.

TC-S03  SCANNER REJECTS POOR CAPTURES                        [NEGATIVE CASE]
================================================================================
Preconditions : On the "Scanner" tab with a paper form.
Test Data     : One form, deliberately mis-presented.

STEPS
 1. Deliberately move the device while capturing, or capture out of focus.
    Expected: The app warns "IMAGE APPEARS BLURRY" rather than accepting an
              unreadable scan.
 2. Confirm what happens after a blur warning.
    Expected: The staff member can retake the photo. A blurry image is not
              silently queued for upload.
 3. Tilt the device noticeably while aiming.
    Expected: The app shows the tilt angle and "HOLD STRAIGHT", warning before
              capture.
 4. Present the form far too small in the frame, or overlapping the frame
    edges.
    Expected: The guidance about fitting the paper inside the frame with a gap
              remains visible, and a badly framed capture is discouraged.
 5. Cover the camera completely and capture.
    Expected: The app reports a problem rather than queueing a black image.
 6. Revoke camera permission in Android settings, then reopen the Scanner tab.
    Expected: A clear message explains the camera is unavailable and how to
              restore it. The app does not crash.
 7. Trigger a camera fault, e.g. open another camera app simultaneously.
    Expected: The failure is reported to the user, not swallowed silently.

Expected Result : Every unusable capture is refused or clearly warned about
                  before it enters the queue, and camera faults are reported
                  rather than hidden.
Actual Result   :
Comments        : The purpose of this case is that GARBAGE NEVER ENTERS THE
                  PIPELINE. A blurry scan that uploads becomes a failed scan
                  someone has to chase later.

TC-S04  SYNC QUEUE - UPLOAD PENDING SCANS                          [LIVE DATA]
================================================================================
Preconditions : At least two scans are queued locally from TC-S02. Network
                available. The n8n workflow is running.
Test Data     : The queued scans. n8n webhook URL from its env var.

STEPS
 1. Open the "Sync Queue" tab.
    Expected: Screen headed "Sync Queue" with "Review and upload scanned
              forms." and an "Upload Pipeline" section. Each queued scan is
              listed.
 2. With nothing queued, read the empty state.
    Expected: Reads "Everything is synced" and "New scans will appear here
              before upload."
 3. Confirm the queue count matches the dashboard.
    Expected: The number of queued items equals the dashboard "PENDING"
              counter.
 4. Tap "Sync All Pending".
    Expected: Status shows "Syncing..." and progresses. It does not hang
              indefinitely with no feedback.
 5. Wait for the upload to finish.
    Expected: Successfully uploaded scans leave the queue. The dashboard
              "PENDING" falls and "SUCCESS" rises by the number uploaded.
 6. Verify the uploaded data landed in the database for the correct term and
    instructor.
    Expected: Each scan's scores are recorded against the right instructor,
              subject and term - the ones printed on the paper form.
 7. Sync again immediately with an empty queue.
    Expected: Handled gracefully. No duplicate records are created.
 8. Scan the SAME physical form twice and sync both.
    Expected: The duplicate is either rejected or flagged for review, NOT
              silently counted twice. Double-counting corrupts an instructor's
              scores.

Expected Result : Queued scans upload with visible progress, leave the queue
                  only on success, land against the correct instructor and
                  term, and duplicates are not silently counted.
Actual Result   :
Comments        : Step 8 matters most for score integrity. Confirm the intended
                  behaviour with the team before judging it pass or fail.

TC-S05  SYNC QUEUE - FAILURES, PAUSE AND REMOVAL         [NEGATIVE, LIVE DATA]
================================================================================
Preconditions : At least two scans queued. Ability to disable the network and
                to stop the n8n workflow.
Test Data     : The queued scans.

STEPS
 1. Disable all network, then tap "Sync All Pending".
    Expected: The upload fails with a clear message such as "Upload failed".
              The scans REMAIN in the queue. Nothing is lost.
 2. Re-enable the network and sync again.
    Expected: The same scans upload successfully. No scan was dropped by the
              earlier failure.
 3. Stop the n8n workflow, then sync.
    Expected: The failure is reported. The scans stay queued rather than being
              marked as sent.
 4. Restart n8n and sync.
    Expected: The scans upload successfully.
 5. Send a payload n8n rejects, e.g. an unreadable image.
    Expected: The scan is not silently dropped. It either stays queued or
              moves to "Failed Scans" (TC-S07) where it can be recovered.
 6. Find the pause control and pause syncing.
    Expected: Status shows "Sync Paused" and no further uploads are attempted
              while paused.
 7. Resume syncing.
    Expected: Uploads continue from where they left off.
 8. Tap "Remove from queue" on one scan.
    Expected: A confirmation appears reading "Remove from queue?". Nothing is
              removed until confirmed.
 9. Cancel that confirmation.
    Expected: The scan stays in the queue.
10. Confirm the removal.
    Expected: The scan leaves the queue, and the "PENDING" counter falls by
              one.
11. Force-close the app with scans still queued, then reopen it.
    Expected: The queued scans are STILL THERE. The queue survives an app
              restart.

Expected Result : Every upload failure leaves the scans safely queued, syncing
                  can be paused and resumed, removal requires confirmation, and
                  the queue survives an app restart.
Actual Result   :
Comments        : Steps 1, 5 and 11 are the core of this case. A scan lost to a
                  network failure or an app restart is unrecoverable, because
                  the paper form has already been handled and set aside.

TC-S06  VALIDATION - CORRECT FLAGGED RECORDS                       [LIVE DATA]
================================================================================
Preconditions : At least one uploaded scan has been flagged, typically because
                the instructor name could not be matched.
Test Data     : One flagged record, with the paper form on hand to check
                against.

STEPS
 1. Open the "Validation" tab.
    Expected: Screen shows "Flagged Records". While loading it reads "Loading
              flagged records…"; with none it reads "No pending verification"
              and "All records are linked to an instructor."
 2. Read a flagged record.
    Expected: Shows the "Student ID" and the date, plus "Instructor Name (Raw)"
              - the text as read off the form - and "Unknown Instructor" where
              no match was found. This is what the staff member needs to
              correct it.
 3. Open "Edit Record".
    Expected: The editable sections appear: "Management Scores (1-5)",
              "Performance Scores (1-5)" and "Remarks & Suggestions" / the
              "Instructor & Remarks" section.
 4. Compare the on-screen scores against the paper form.
    Expected: Every score matches what the student actually marked. This is the
              point of validation.
 5. Enter a score outside 1-5.
    Expected: Refused. The section is labelled "(1-5)" and must enforce it.
 6. Correct the instructor and save.
    Expected: The record saves. On failure the message reads "Error saving:"
              rather than appearing to succeed.
 7. Tap "Approve & Sync".
    Expected: Message reads "Data updated and queued for processing." and the
              record leaves the flagged list.
 8. Confirm the corrected record reached the right instructor.
    Expected: The scores now appear against the instructor named on the paper
              form, for the correct term.
 9. On another flagged record, tap "Discard".
    Expected: A confirmation is required. On confirming, the message reads
              "Record discarded." and it leaves the list.
10. Confirm what discarding actually did.
    Expected: The discarded record does NOT contribute to any instructor's
              scores. Confirm with the team whether the raw scan is retained
              for audit - a discard that erases the only copy of a student's
              evaluation needs to be a deliberate decision, not a surprise.

Expected Result : Flagged records show enough raw detail to be corrected
                  against the paper form, scores are constrained to 1-5,
                  approving routes the data to the correct instructor, and
                  discarding is confirmed and understood.
Actual Result   :
Comments        : Step 4 is the whole reason this screen exists. Step 10 should
                  be settled with the team before the defense, because a
                  panelist may well ask it.

TC-S07  FAILED SCANS - RECOVERY QUEUE
================================================================================
Preconditions : At least one scan has failed detection, e.g. from TC-S05
                step 5.
Test Data     : One failed scan.

STEPS
 1. Open "Failed Scans" from the Validation tab.
    Expected: Screen titled "Failed Scans" with a "Recovery Queue". While
              loading it reads "Loading failed scans…"; with none it reads "No
              failed scans" and "Every scan has been processed successfully."
 2. Read a failed scan row.
    Expected: Shows the reason for failure - "Grid detection failed",
              "Table/corners not detected" or "Detection issue" - plus
              "Instructor:" and "Student ID:" where known, and "Unknown" or
              "Unknown date" where not.
 3. Confirm the reason is specific enough to act on.
    Expected: The staff member can tell WHY it failed and therefore whether
              re-scanning would help. A bare "failed" with no reason is not
              enough.
 4. Open a failed scan's detail.
    Expected: The detail view opens, showing the captured image or enough
              information to identify which paper form it was.
 5. Establish whether the scan can be recovered.
    Expected: There is a path to either correct and resubmit it, or to
              re-scan the original form. A failed scan must not be a dead end,
              because the paper form may already have been filed away.
 6. Recover one failed scan and confirm the outcome.
    Expected: It leaves the recovery queue and its data reaches the correct
              instructor.
 7. Count the failed scans against the totals.
    Expected: Scans that failed are NOT counted as successfully gathered on
              the dashboard or on the admin's "Files Scanned" figure.

Expected Result : Every failed scan is listed with a specific, actionable reason
                  and can be recovered, and failed scans are never counted as
                  successful.
Actual Result   :
Comments        : Step 7 protects the integrity of the reported totals. Step 5
                  determines whether a failure means real data loss.

TC-S08  IMPORT FROM GOOGLE SHEETS                                  [LIVE DATA]
================================================================================
Preconditions : A prepared Google Sheet of evaluation data. The n8n import
                workflow is running.
Test Data     : One prepared sheet, including one deliberately bad row.

STEPS
 1. On the dashboard, tap "IMPORT FROM GOOGLE SHEETS".
    Expected: The import screen opens and states what it expects, rather than
              presenting an unexplained blank form.
 2. Start an import with the prepared sheet.
    Expected: Progress is shown, and the import ends with a clear success or
              failure message - never an indefinite spinner.
 3. Cross-check the imported rows against the sheet.
    Expected: Every row is either imported or reported as an error. Nothing is
              silently skipped.
 4. Confirm the deliberately bad row was caught.
    Expected: It is reported rather than imported, and appears under the
              staff's own "Import Errors" tab.
 5. Confirm the imported data landed against the correct term.
    Expected: The records belong to the active term, not a previous one.
 6. Import the SAME sheet a second time.
    Expected: Duplicates are rejected or flagged, NOT counted twice.
 7. Run the import with n8n stopped.
    Expected: A clear failure. No partial import is left behind.
 8. Check the dashboard counters after a successful import.
    Expected: The term total increases by the number of rows actually
              imported, and by no more.

Expected Result : A sheet imports with visible progress and an explicit
                  outcome, bad rows are reported rather than dropped,
                  re-importing does not double-count, and the counters reflect
                  exactly what was imported.
Actual Result   :
Comments        : Step 6 is the one most likely to fail quietly and the most
                  damaging if it does.

TC-S09  STAFF SETTINGS AND END OF SHIFT
================================================================================
Preconditions : Signed in as SAO staff. Access to the account's inbox for the
                email change.
Test Data     : A new password of at least six characters, NOT recorded in this
                document.

STEPS
 1. Open the gatherer Settings.
    Expected: The screen loads (briefly "Loading gatherer settings…") and
              identifies the role as "Data Gatherer". The security section
              reads "Email, password, and account controls."
 2. Tap "Edit Personal Details", change the name and save.
    Expected: The profile saves and the new name appears in the drawer.
 3. Toggle "Haptic Feedback" off and on.
    Expected: The setting changes and persists after leaving and reopening
              Settings.
 4. Tap "Edit Email" and submit a valid new address.
    Expected: Message reads "Email updated! Check your inbox for
              confirmation." The old address keeps working until the link is
              clicked.
 5. Tap "Edit Password" and submit mismatched passwords.
    Expected: Refused with "Passwords do not match."
 6. Submit a password shorter than six characters.
    Expected: Refused with "Password must be at least 6 characters."
 7. Submit a fully valid password change.
    Expected: Message reads "Password updated successfully. Please log in
              again." and the app signs out. On failure it reads "Failed to
              update password."
 8. Sign in with the old password, then the new one.
    Expected: The old password is rejected; the new one is accepted.
 9. Queue at least one unsynced scan, then tap "End Shift & Log Out".
    Expected: A confirmation is required. IMPORTANT: the staff member should be
              warned that scans are still pending, or the scans must survive
              the logout. Ending a shift must not silently discard queued work.
10. Complete the logout, sign back in, and check the Sync Queue.
    Expected: The pending scans are still there and can be synced.
11. Open "Deactivate My Account", then CANCEL.
    Expected: A warning explains the account can only be restored by the SAO
              Admin, and cancelling leaves the account active.

Expected Result : The staff member can maintain their own account with every
                  password rule enforced, and ending a shift never loses
                  queued scans.
Actual Result   :
Comments        : Steps 9 and 10 are the important ones. "End Shift & Log Out"
                  is exactly when a tired user is most likely to lose unsynced
                  work.

  Items 1 to 4 are about DATA LOSS. A paper form is handled once, so a scan
  lost anywhere in this pipeline is gone for good. Treat any failure in those
  four as the highest-severity defect in the project, ahead of anything
  cosmetic.
