                         Credentials are deliberately NOT recorded here.

WHAT THIS ROLE DOES
  The Department Head reviews the faculty in their OWN department, spots who is
  struggling, and takes formal action through an intervention report. Two
  properties matter more than anything cosmetic:

    1. SCOPE  - a head sees only their own department, never the whole college.
    2. EFFECT - an intervention they raise reaches the instructor, and an
                instructor cannot dismiss it.

  This role can also deactivate an instructor's account, so that path is tested
  explicitly.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO READ THIS DOCUMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Text in "double quotes" appears on screen exactly as written.
* Names in [square brackets] are database tables or services to check when a
  step needs back-end verification.
* Each step has its own expected result, so a failed case identifies WHICH
  step failed.
* "Actual Result" is blank where the case has not been run.

!! WARNING !!
  Cases marked [LIVE DATA] create real intervention records against real
  instructors, or deactivate a real account. Run them on staging, or on a
  throwaway instructor account, and never during a live evaluation period.
  TC-D08 (deactivating an instructor) is the highest-impact action in this
  module.

TC-D01  SIGN IN AS DEPARTMENT HEAD AND READ THE EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
================================================================================
Preconditions : App on the Login screen. Account is active with role
                DEPARTMENT_HEAD or DEAN, and IS assigned to a department.
Test Data     : View only. Tables [department_table], [user_info].

STEPS
 1. Sign in with the department head credentials.
    Expected: Routes to the department head landing screen, NOT the instructor
              or admin dashboard. Bottom tabs read "Overview", "Faculty",
              "Subjects", "Reports".
 2. Confirm the role boundary.
    Expected: There is NO route to any SAO Admin screen - no Users, no
              Personnel, no System Settings. A head who can reach an admin
              screen is a FAIL.
 3. Read the header and leadership area.
    Expected: Shows "Executive Overview" / "Department Overview" and identifies
              the department this head leads, under "Department Leadership".
 4. Read the summary figures.
    Expected: Shows "Department Average", "Active Faculty" and "Total Forms
              Processed". The average is qualified by "Based on N of M
              faculty", so a partly-evaluated department is not mistaken for a
              complete one.
 5. Confirm the department average only covers THIS department.
    Expected: The figure reconciles with this department's instructors only.
              No instructor from another department contributes.
 6. Read "Department Growth".
    Expected: Subtitle reads "Weighted mean across recent academic terms." with
              a value per term. With no history it reads "No historical data
              available yet".
 7. Read "Department Voice (AI Analysis)".
    Expected: Subtitle reads "Aggregated themes from all student comments
              across the college." With no data it reads "No feedback data yet
              for this term."
 8. Read the "Action Required" section.
    Expected: Subtitle reads "Faculty and curriculum signals that need review."
              With nothing flagged it reads "No critical issues detected."
 9. Sign in as a department head with NO department assigned.
    Expected: The screen explains the account has no department rather than
              showing an empty or misleading dashboard, and does not crash.

Expected Result : The head lands on their own overview with no admin access,
                  and every figure is scoped to their department alone.
Actual Result   :
Comments        : Step 5 is the substance of this case. A head seeing another
                  department's figures is a confidentiality failure, not a
                  cosmetic bug.
                  Step 7's subtitle says "across the college" while the rest of
                  the screen is department-scoped. Confirm with the team which
                  is intended - if the AI analysis really is college-wide on a
                  department screen, the wording or the scope is wrong.

TC-D02  FACULTY ROSTER - SEARCH, SORT AND FLAGGED FACULTY
================================================================================
Preconditions : Signed in as department head with several instructors in the
                department, at least one scoring below the intervention
                threshold.
Test Data     : View only. Table [user_info] filtered by department.

STEPS
 1. Open the "Faculty" tab.
    Expected: Screen titled "Faculty Roster". While loading it reads "Loading
              faculty roster…"; with none it reads "No instructors yet" and
              "Faculty assigned to this department will appear here."
 2. Confirm who is listed.
    Expected: ONLY instructors assigned to this head's department. Cross-check
              against [user_info] for that department ID.
 3. Read one faculty row.
    Expected: Shows the instructor's name, "Overall Score", "Total Evals" and a
              sentiment split across "Positive", "Neutral" and "Negative".
 4. Find an instructor scoring below the threshold.
    Expected: They carry an "Intervention Required" marker, so a head can spot
              who needs attention without reading every score.
 5. Type a partial name into "Search instructor name...".
    Expected: The list narrows to matching instructors. Searching by university
              ID also works.
 6. Search for a name that does not exist.
    Expected: Reads "No matching instructors" and "Try a different name or
              university ID." - not a blank screen.
 7. Tap "Clear Search".
    Expected: The full roster returns.
 8. Search for an instructor who belongs to ANOTHER department.
    Expected: They are NOT found. Search must not reach outside the
              department.
 9. Apply each sort option: "Name (A-Z)", "Score (Highest to Lowest)",
    "Score (Lowest to Highest)".
    Expected: Each reorders the list correctly, and the sort survives a search
              being applied and cleared.
10. Tap a faculty row.
    Expected: The instructor detail page opens for that person.

Expected Result : The roster lists only this department's faculty, flags who
                  needs intervention, and search and sort work without ever
                  exposing another department's staff.
Actual Result   :
Comments        : Step 8 is the confidentiality check.

TC-D03  INSTRUCTOR DETAIL - PERFORMANCE AND SENTIMENT
================================================================================
Preconditions : An instructor with evaluation data is open from the roster.
Test Data     : View only. Tables [overall_total_survey],
                [management_results], [student_remarks], [instructor_subjects].

STEPS
 1. Open an instructor from the roster.
    Expected: The profile loads (briefly "Loading instructor profile…") and
              names the instructor.
 2. Read the score summary.
    Expected: Shows "Management", "Performance" and "Evaluations" for the
              active term.
 3. Cross-check those figures against what the INSTRUCTOR sees on their own
    dashboard.
    Expected: Both roles report the SAME numbers for the same instructor and
              term. A discrepancy here undermines every conversation the head
              will have with that instructor.
 4. Read "Performance Trend".
    Expected: Subtitle reads "Historical score across all evaluation terms"
              with a value per term. With none it reads "No historical data
              available."
 5. Read the "Sentiment Analysis" area.
    Expected: Shows "Comment Polarity Distribution" across "Positive",
              "Neutral", "Negative" / "Critical", plus "Key Sentiment Tags".
 6. Cross-check the sentiment split against [student_remarks] for that
    instructor and term.
    Expected: The percentages reflect the stored tone values.
 7. Read the subject list.
    Expected: The instructor's subjects for this term are listed. With none it
              reads "No subjects this term" and "Assigned subjects will appear
              here when available."
 8. Confirm every verbal description matches its band.
    Expected: "Outstanding" >=4.20, "Satisfactory" >=2.60, and so on, matching
              the same bands used elsewhere in the app.
 9. Open an instructor with no evaluations yet.
    Expected: Reads "No Data Yet" / "No data" rather than showing zeros as if
              they were real scores.

Expected Result : The head sees a complete and accurate picture of one
                  instructor, and every figure agrees with what that instructor
                  sees themselves.
Actual Result   :
Comments        : Step 3 is the important one. Two roles disagreeing about the
                  same instructor's score is a serious data defect.

TC-D04  DRAFT AN INTERVENTION REPORT                               [LIVE DATA]
================================================================================
Preconditions : Signed in as department head. One flagged instructor, ideally a
                throwaway account. Table [intervention_reports], written via
                [evaluation_service].
Test Data     : One intervention against a throwaway instructor.

STEPS
 1. Open the "Reports" tab.
    Expected: Screen titled "Intervention Reports" with "Manage formal actions
              and track support for flagged faculty." While loading it reads
              "Loading interventions…".
 2. Read the pending and history areas.
    Expected: Pending items appear under a "Pending Action Required" heading;
              resolved ones under "Intervention Log". With nothing pending it
              reads "All caught up" and "No pending interventions require
              action."; with no history, "No intervention history" and
              "Completed and resolved interventions will appear here."
 3. Read a flagged alert.
    Expected: Shows "Instructor:" and "Flagged for:" with the reason, plus the
              date it was flagged, so the head knows why the system raised it.
 4. Tap "Draft Intervention Report" (also reachable from the Faculty Roster).
    Expected: A form opens to record a formal action against that instructor.
 5. Open the action-type selector.
    Expected: The available actions include "Peer Mentoring Program",
              "Pedagogy & Teaching Workshop", "Curriculum Review Meeting",
              "Faculty Support", "Mandated Action" and "Formal Administrative
              Warning".
 6. Submit the form with no action type chosen.
    Expected: Refused. A formal record must state what action was mandated.
 7. Complete the form with an action and notes, then submit.
    Expected: Message reads "Intervention recorded successfully." and it
              appears in the pending list marked "PENDING".
 8. Submit an intervention with the notes left empty.
    Expected: Either it is refused, or the record clearly reads "No notes
              provided." rather than showing an empty gap.
 9. Confirm the record in [intervention_reports].
    Expected: One row exists, naming the correct instructor, the acting head
              as author, the action type, and the creation date.
10. Open "Intervention Report Details" on the new record.
    Expected: Shows "Action:", the notes, "Created:" with the date, and the
              instructor's name.
11. Draft a second intervention for the SAME instructor.
    Expected: Handled sensibly - either allowed as a separate record or
              refused with a reason. It must not create a corrupt or duplicate
              row.

Expected Result : A formal intervention can be raised against a flagged
                  instructor with a mandated action type recorded, and it is
                  stored and listed as pending.

TC-D05  THE INSTRUCTOR RECEIVES THE INTERVENTION       [CROSS-ROLE, LIVE DATA]
================================================================================
Preconditions : The intervention from TC-D04 exists. A SECOND device signed in
                as that instructor, with their dashboard open.
Test Data     : The intervention raised in TC-D04.

WHY THIS CASE EXISTS
  An intervention that the instructor never sees is useless, and one the
  instructor can delete is not a formal record. This case tests both halves of
  that, across two roles at once.

STEPS
 1. With the instructor's dashboard already open and NOT restarted, raise the
    intervention from the head's device.
    Expected: The instructor's dashboard updates by itself. The app subscribes
              to live changes on [intervention_reports], so no manual refresh
              or restart should be needed.
 2. Read what appears on the instructor's dashboard.
    Expected: A card headed "Official Notice — Action Required" appears at the
              top, showing "From:", "Action:" and "Notes:", plus the date.
 3. Confirm the content matches what the head submitted.
    Expected: The action type and notes are exactly what was recorded in
              TC-D04, attributed to the correct department head.
 4. Read the instructor's notification bell.
    Expected: A count badge appears, and the bell reports the active notice
              rather than staying silent.
 5. As the INSTRUCTOR, attempt to dismiss, delete or hide the notice.
    Expected: There is NO such control. A lock icon carries the tooltip "Only
              your department head can remove this notice".
 6. Restart the instructor's app.
    Expected: The notice is still there. It is not a transient message.
 7. Confirm a DIFFERENT instructor does not see this notice.
    Expected: The notice appears only for the instructor it was raised
              against.

Expected Result : An intervention raised by the head reaches the correct
                  instructor live, states what was mandated and by whom,
                  persists across restarts, and cannot be removed by the
                  instructor.
Actual Result   :
Comments        : This is the single most important case in the module, because
                  it is the only one that proves the intervention feature
                  actually does its job end to end. Steps 5 and 7 are the
                  integrity checks.

TC-D06  RESOLVE AN INTERVENTION                                    [LIVE DATA]
================================================================================
Preconditions : The pending intervention from TC-D04 exists. The instructor's
                device is available to observe the effect.
Test Data     : That intervention.

STEPS
 1. On the "Reports" tab, open the pending intervention.
    Expected: It is marked "PENDING" and offers "Mark as Resolved".
 2. Tap "Mark as Resolved".
    Expected: A confirmation is required before a formal record changes state.
 3. Confirm the action.
    Expected: Message reads "Intervention marked as resolved." and it moves
              from the pending list into the "Intervention Log".
 4. Confirm the pending area updates.
    Expected: With nothing else outstanding it reads "All caught up".
 5. On the INSTRUCTOR's device, observe their dashboard.
    Expected: The "Official Notice — Action Required" card is removed, or is
              clearly shown as resolved. A resolved intervention must not keep
              demanding action from the instructor.
 6. Confirm the record still exists in history.
    Expected: The resolved intervention remains in the "Intervention Log" as a
              historical record. Resolving is not deleting.
 7. Confirm the instructor still cannot alter it.
    Expected: The instructor has no control over the resolved record either.

Expected Result : A pending intervention can be resolved behind a confirmation,
                  the instructor's notice clears, and the record is retained in
                  history rather than deleted.
Actual Result   :
Comments        : Step 6 matters for auditability. A resolved intervention that
                  disappears entirely destroys the department's record of
                  action taken.

TC-D07  SUBJECT ANALYTICS - CURRICULUM HEALTH
================================================================================
Preconditions : Signed in as department head with evaluated subjects in the
                active term.
Test Data     : View only. Table [instructor_subjects] and the results tables.

STEPS
 1. Open the "Subjects" tab.
    Expected: Screen titled "Subject Analytics". While loading it reads
              "Loading curriculum analytics…"; with none it reads "No subject
              data" and "Analytics will appear after this term receives
              evaluations."
 2. Read the "Subject Health" section.
    Expected: Subtitle reads "Performance patterns that may require review or
              teaching support." with a row per subject.
 3. Read one subject row.
    Expected: Shows "Avg Score" and a comparison under "Score vs. Department
              Average", including "Dept Avg:" so the head can see whether a
              subject is above or below the department.
 4. Confirm the subjects listed belong to this department.
    Expected: No subject from another department appears.
 5. Read the flag categories on a problem subject.
    Expected: Flags distinguish the KIND of problem - "Curriculum",
              "Difficulty", "Sentiment", "System Anomaly", "Critical" - rather
              than a single undifferentiated warning.
 6. Confirm a "System Anomaly" flag means what it says.
    Expected: It indicates a data problem, e.g. an implausible or incomplete
              result, not a teaching problem. A data fault must not be
              presented to a head as poor teaching.
 7. Tap a subject row ("Tap to view instructor breakdown").
    Expected: An "Instructor Performance Breakdown" opens showing who teaches
              that subject and how each performs.
 8. Cross-check one subject's average against its instructors' scores for that
    subject.
    Expected: The figures reconcile.

Expected Result : Subject-level analytics are scoped to the department,
                  distinguish curriculum problems from data anomalies, and
                  reconcile with the per-instructor figures.
Actual Result   :
Comments        : Step 6 is worth settling with the team. A head acting on a
                  "System Anomaly" as if it were a performance problem could
                  lead to an unfair intervention.

TC-D08  DEACTIVATE AN INSTRUCTOR ACCOUNT                           [LIVE DATA]
================================================================================
Preconditions : A THROWAWAY instructor account in this department that can
                currently sign in. Arrange reactivation with an SAO admin
                beforehand.
Test Data     : That account's credentials, held by the tester.

!! HIGHEST-IMPACT ACTION IN THIS MODULE !!
   A department head can lock an instructor out of the system. Only an SAO
   admin can reverse it. Never run this against a real instructor.

STEPS
 1. Open a throwaway instructor from the roster and find "Deactivate Account".
    Expected: The control is present but not something that fires on a single
              stray tap.
 2. Tap it.
    Expected: A dialog titled "Deactivate Account?" appears, asking "Are you
              sure you want to deactivate {instructor name}", naming the
              actual instructor so the wrong person cannot be deactivated by
              accident.
 3. Cancel the dialog.
    Expected: Nothing changes and the account still signs in.
 4. Confirm with "Deactivate".
    Expected: Message reads "Account deactivated successfully." and the
              instructor is shown as deactivated.
 5. Attempt to sign in as that instructor.
    Expected: Refused with "Your account has been deactivated by the
              administration." They reach no screen inside the app.
 6. Confirm the instructor's evaluation data survives.
    Expected: Scores and remarks are retained. This is a deactivation, not a
              deletion.
 7. Confirm the action was recorded.
    Expected: The deactivation appears in the SAO admin's "Security Audit
              Logs", attributed to this department head. A head deactivating an
              account with NO audit trail is a serious accountability gap.
 8. Attempt to deactivate an instructor from ANOTHER department.
    Expected: Not possible. A head's authority stops at their own department.
 9. Force a failure, e.g. deactivate with no network.
    Expected: Message reads "Failed to deactivate:" and the account is NOT
              left half-deactivated.
10. Have an SAO admin reactivate the account.
    Expected: Succeeds, and the instructor can sign in again.

Expected Result : A head can deactivate an instructor in their own department
                  behind a named confirmation, the account is then locked out,
                  its data survives, the action is audited, and it can be
                  reversed by an admin.
Actual Result   :
Comments        : OPEN QUESTION FOR THE TEAM: this flow appears to have NO
                  emailed verification code, whereas the SAO admin needs one to
                  create a privileged account. Deactivating a colleague's
                  account is comparably consequential. Confirm whether the
                  absence of a code here is a deliberate design decision. If it
                  is not, raise it as a defect.
                  Step 10 is mandatory - leave the account as you found it.

================================================================================
Preconditions : An instructor with evaluation data is open from the roster.
Test Data     : View and export.

STEPS
 1. From the instructor detail page, open their official evaluation report.
    Expected: The screen titled "Official Evaluation Report" opens for THAT
              instructor.
 2. Check the header identity fields.

    Expected: A PDF is written and the share sheet opens. Its contents match
              the screen, and its "Academic Year" also reads correctly.
 7. Confirm the report is for the correct term.
    Expected: The academic year matches the term the head is reviewing, not a

Expected Result : The head can open and export the correct instructor's
                  official report, with identity and term fields rendered
                  correctly in both the screen and the PDF.

TC-D10  EXECUTIVE SETTINGS - PROFILE, ALERTS AND SECURITY
================================================================================
Preconditions : Signed in as department head, Settings opened from the drawer.
                Access to the account's inbox.
Test Data     : A new password of at least six characters, NOT recorded in this
                document.

STEPS
 1. Open "Account Settings" from the drawer.
    Expected: Screen titled "Executive Settings" (briefly "Loading executive
              settings…"), showing "Executive Profile" and identifying the role
              as "Dept Head" with the department and "College".
 2. Tap "Edit Personal Details" / "Edit Executive Profile", change the name and
    save.
    Expected: The profile saves and the new name appears in the drawer header.
 3. Find the "Executive Intelligence" section.
    Expected: Subtitle reads "Choose which department signals should notify
              you." with two toggles: "Alert when instructor rating drops below
              3.0." and "Alert on detected negative student feedback."
 4. Toggle each alert off, leave Settings, and return.
    Expected: Both settings persist. On failure the message reads "Failed to
              update" naming the setting, rather than silently reverting.
 5. With the rating alert ON, arrange for an instructor to fall below 3.0.
    Expected: The head is alerted, and the instructor appears flagged on the
              roster and under "Action Required".
 6. With the rating alert OFF, repeat.
    Expected: No alert is raised, confirming the toggle actually controls
              behaviour rather than only appearing to.
 7. Tap "Edit Email" and submit a valid new address.
    Expected: Reads "Enter your new email address. You will receive a
              confirmation link.", then "Email updated! Check your inbox for
              confirmation." The old address works until the link is clicked.
 8. Tap "Edit Password" and submit mismatched passwords.
    Expected: Refused with a mismatch message.
 9. Submit a valid new password with the WRONG current password.
    Expected: Refused. The password does not change.
10. Submit a fully valid password change.
    Expected: Succeeds and the session ends, requiring a fresh sign-in. On
              failure it reads "Failed to update password."
11. Open "Deactivate My Account", then CANCEL.
    Expected: A warning explains the account can only be restored by the SAO
              Admin, and cancelling leaves the account active.

Expected Result : The head can maintain their own profile, the two alert
                  toggles genuinely control whether they are notified, and
                  every password rule is enforced.
Actual Result   :
Comments        : Steps 5 and 6 are the real test. A settings toggle that saves
                  but changes nothing is a common and easily missed defect.

  intervention feature works at all. Item 7 is accountability. Treat failures
  in those ahead of anything cosmetic.

  TWO OPEN QUESTIONS FOR THE TEAM, both raised in the cases above:
    a) TC-D08 - a department head can deactivate an instructor with no emailed
       verification code, while an SAO admin needs one to create a privileged
       account. Is that asymmetry intended?
    b) TC-D01 - "Department Voice (AI Analysis)" is subtitled "across the
       college" on an otherwise department-scoped screen. Is the scope or the
       wording wrong?
